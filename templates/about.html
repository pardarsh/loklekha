About Loklekha

Loklekha is a public issue ledger. People record public problems, attach evidence, discuss remedies, and follow institutional responses, decisions, actions and outcomes over time. Every change is attributed and kept in history.

Status is not outcome

An issue's status is an administrative label set by the responsible body or by moderators. Reported resolved means the responsible body says the work is complete; it is not independent verification. Observed outcomes, completion claims and disputes are recorded separately so readers can compare them.

Evidence and sources

Links and documents supplied by users are marked as user-submitted. Official publications are evidence of what an institution published, not proof that every statement in them is true. Links between records carry a certainty: unverified, asserted, sourced or disputed.

Corrections and redaction

Records are corrected by adding to their history, not by rewriting it. Personal data can be redacted by moderators, which removes it from the record and its history while keeping a note that a redaction happened.

Moderation

Anyone with an account can report a record. Moderators may hide records, redact personal data and keep private notes. Popularity is not proof of truth.

Privacy

Do not publish personal information, exact home addresses or anything that could put someone at risk. Reporters may withhold their identity from the public; it is retained for moderation.
